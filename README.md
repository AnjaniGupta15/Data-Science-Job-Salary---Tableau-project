# 📊 Data Science Job Salary Analysis – Tableau Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a Tableau dashboard created to analyze Data Science job
salaries and understand salary patterns across different job-related
categories.

The dashboard analyzes salary based on **experience level, employment type,
job title, company size, company location, and employee residence**.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective of this project is to:

- Analyze average salaries across different experience levels
- Compare salaries across employment types
- Analyze companies based on their size and location
- Explore employee residence by country
- Compare average salary across different job titles
- Present the analysis through an interactive Tableau dashboard

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel** – Data preparation and basic data exploration
- **Tableau** – Data analysis and visualization
- **Data Visualization**

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains information about Data Science and Technology-related
jobs, including:

- Job Title
- Experience Level
- Employment Type
- Salary
- Employee Residence
- Company Location
- Company Size

---

## 📊 Dashboard

The dashboard contains the following visualizations:

### 1. Average Salary by Experience and Employment Type
Shows average salary across different experience levels for Contract,
Freelance, Full Time and Part Time employment types.

### 2. Total Companies by Size and Location
Shows the distribution of companies according to their company size.

### 3. Experience Level
Shows the distribution of employees across different experience levels.

### 4. Employment Type
Shows the distribution of different employment types.

### 5. Top 10 Employee Residence
Shows the top countries based on employee residence.

### 6. Map – Average Salary by Country
Displays average salary across different company locations using a map.

### 7. Average Salary by Experience and Job Title
Provides a detailed comparison of average salary across job titles and
experience levels.

---

## 🎛️ Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters for:

- **Company Location**
- **Job Title**

These filters allow users to explore the dashboard based on their selected
location or job role.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Medium-sized companies** represent the largest share of companies in the
  dashboard.
- **Senior-level employees** represent the largest experience-level group.
- The **United States** has the highest number of employee residence records
  among the countries shown in the Top 10 chart.
- Average salary varies across different **job titles and experience levels**.
- The map shows differences in average salary across **company locations**.
